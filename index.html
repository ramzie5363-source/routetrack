How it works

Just four simple steps

Add your route

Paste a Google Maps directions link, or type your start/stops, or drop a KML / GPX / GeoJSON file.

Build the route

Stops are located and joined by a road-following line, with Google or OpenStreetMap routing.

Check the preview

See the line on a map, with distance, duration and stops, before you export anything.

Download

GPX, Shapefile (.zip), GeoJSON, KML or CSV – one at a time or all together.

In Google Maps: choose Directions, add your start, stops and end, then copy the address from the browser’s address bar. Short maps.app.goo.gl links must be opened first so you can copy the long address.

Use Google routing (optional API key)

Without a key the line is calculated with OpenStreetMap routing between your stops – very close to Google’s, but not always identical. With a Google Maps Platform key that has the Routes API enabled, the line comes from Google itself.

About

Private by design

Conversion happens in your browser. Files are never uploaded. Only your stops are sent to the routing and geocoding service (OpenStreetMap, or Google if you add a key). A key is kept in your browser only if you tick “Remember”.

About accuracy

Google doesn’t offer a free route download, so the line is rebuilt between your stops. Add a Google key for Google’s own geometry. Check the preview before using it for survey or patrol records.

Other formats

Need GeoPackage, DXF or something else? Open the GeoJSON or Shapefile in QGIS and use Export → Save Features As….
